# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)

**Current Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Ticker:** NSE: SRVCABLE  

---

### 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. In its official Q1 FY27 results (quarter ended 30 June 2026), the company reported consolidated revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% YoY), EBITDA of ₹140 crore (up 2.6% YoY), and Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹82 crore (up 5.9% YoY) [S3]. Management maintains full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% [S2]. Note: A local news report also covers a penalty on a local cable TV/broadband entity named "Sarvottam Cable Network" [S6], which appears to be an unrelated entity.

---

### 2. Bull Case
- **Healthy Order Book and Expansion:** The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3]. The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant will add roughly 30% to cable capacity and is slated to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3], with approximately 70% of its ₹620 crore capex already incurred [S2].
- **High-Growth Segments:** Data-centre cables represent approximately 9% of domestic revenue, with management projecting this share could double in two years [S2]. Export revenue grew to 21% of total revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
- **De-Pledging and Institutional Backing:** Promoter pledge levels have dropped steadily from a historical high of 35% in March 2024 [S4] down to 4.1% of promoter holdings as of June 2026 [S8]. Combined institutional ownership (FII + DII) increased to 26.4% in June 2026 from 21.9% in June 2025 [S8].
- **Sound Leverage Profile:** Management reported a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35x as capex is primarily internally funded [S2].

---

### 3. Bear Case
- **Margin Compression & Commodity Sensitivity:** EBITDA margins fell 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, caused by copper prices rising nearly 14% [S1, S3]. While roughly two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses, resets occur with a lag of one to two quarters [S2].
- **Deteriorating Working Capital:** Working capital days have lengthened; receivable days expanded from 78 to 96 days YoY, primarily driven by slow collections from two large state utility clients [S2].
- **Disputed Regulatory Liability:** The company received a CGST demand order on 2 September 2026 totaling ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalties) regarding input tax credits for FY21 and FY22 [S7]. This represents more than half of Q1 FY27 net profit (₹82 crore) [S3, S7].

---

### 4. Open Questions & Data Discrepancies
- **Revenue Discrepancy:** The company's official press release reports Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S3], whereas a news article [S1] and an unverified retail stock blog [S5] state revenue was ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY). 
- **Receivables Risk:** Details are lacking on the exact exposure to the two slow-paying state utilities and whether provisions or write-downs may be required if delays persist [S2].
- **Tax Dispute Outcome:** The likelihood and timing of the appeal against the ₹46.3 crore GST demand order remain uncertain [S7].
- **Audit Status:** The supplied documents do not clarify whether the Q1 FY27 figures are audited or unaudited [S1, S2, S3].
