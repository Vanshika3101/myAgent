# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) — Investment Research Brief

**As of:** 23 September 2026  

---

### 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. According to the company's Q1 FY27 press release, consolidated revenue from operations grew 18.0% year-on-year to ₹1,248 crore (though a media report cited ₹1,428 crore / +35% [S1, S3]), while Profit After Tax (PAT) grew 5.9% year-on-year to ₹82 crore [S3]. EBITDA margins contracted 170 basis points to 11.2% due to rising copper raw material costs [S1, S3]. 

---

### 2. Bull Case
* **Healthy Order Book and Demand Growth:** The company's order book expanded by 23.8% year-on-year to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 (up from ₹3,150 crore) [S3], driven by power transmission, utility, and data-centre demand [S1, S2, S3].
* **Capacity Expansion on Track:** Commercial production at the new ₹620 crore Bharuch plant is scheduled for Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity [S2, S3]. Management noted that around 70% of capex is already spent, funded primarily via internal accruals and term debt, maintaining net debt to equity at a modest 0.35x [S2].
* **High-Margin Niche Expansion & Exports:** Data-centre cables represent approximately 9% of domestic revenue, with management targeting a doubling within two years [S2]. Export contribution rose to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, up from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
* **Substantial Reduction in Promoter Pledging:** Pledged promoter shares dropped significantly to 4.1% of promoter holdings as of June 2026 (down from 11.5% in June 2025 and 35% in March 2024), reducing structural governance risks [S4, S8]. Institutional ownership (FII and DII) has simultaneously trended upward to 26.4% [S8].

---

### 3. Bear Case
* **Margin Pressure & Raw Material Pass-Through Lag:** EBITDA margins fell 170 bps to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to an approximate 14% quarterly increase in copper prices [S1, S3]. While roughly two-thirds of contracts contain price-variation clauses, management noted that these reset with a lag of one to two quarters [S2].
* **Working Capital Deterioration:** Debtor days stretched from 78 to 96 days year-on-year, driven by slower collections from two large state utility customers [S2].
* **Substantial Tax Dispute Contingency:** The company received a CGST demand order on 2 September 2026 for ₹46.3 crore (inclusive of interest and penalty) regarding input tax credits for FY21 and FY22 [S7]. This represents over 56% of Q1 FY27 PAT (₹82 crore) [S3, S7].
* **Growth Deceleration Guidance:** Management guided for full-year FY27 revenue growth of 15% to 17% [S2], which represents a moderation from Q1 reported growth rates [S1, S3].

---

### 4. Open Questions
* **Revenue Discrepancy:** The company's official filing states Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S3], whereas *Business Daily* and an unverified retail blog report ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S1, S5]. The reason for this ₹180 crore divergence requires verification with audited financial statements.
* **Audit Status of Reported Results:** The supplied filings do not confirm whether the Q1 FY27 results are audited or limited-review unaudited figures [S1, S3].
* **Working Capital Resolution:** It remains unclear whether payment delays from the two state utility customers will worsen or lead to provisions [S2].
* **Outcome of Tax Appeal:** Management intends to appeal the ₹46.3 crore GST demand [S7], but potential escrow deposit requirements and ultimate financial liability remain uncertain.
* **Entity Distinction:** A news report noted an ₹8 lakh civic penalty against "Sarvottam Cable Network" (owned by Suresh Patil) in Nagpur [S6]; it is unclear whether this entity is affiliated with Sarvottam Cables Ltd, though it appears to be an unrelated local cable TV operator [S3, S6].
