# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)

**As of:** 23 September 2026  

---

### 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), the company reported consolidated revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), EBITDA of ₹140 crore (+2.6% YoY), and profit after tax (PAT) of ₹82 crore (+5.9% YoY) [S3]. Operating/EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% due to a ~14% increase in copper input costs [S1, S3]. 

*(Note: Third-party media reported Q1 revenue as ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S1], but official company exchange filings state ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S3].)*

---

### 2. Bull Case
* **Healthy Order Book and Demand Drivers:** The company’s order book expanded 23.8% YoY to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026 (up from ₹3,150 crore) [S3], supported by demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S1, S3]. Data-centre cables represent ~9% of domestic revenue, with management projecting this share to double within two years [S2].
* **Capacity Expansion on Schedule:** The new Bharuch (Gujarat) manufacturing facility will expand cable capacity by approximately 30% and is scheduled to begin commercial operations in Q3 FY27, contributing to H2 FY27 revenue [S2, S3]. Capex is ₹620 crore (~70% completed), funded via internal accruals and term debt [S2].
* **Growing Export Mix:** Export revenue rose to 21% of total revenue in Q1 FY27, compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3].
* **De-pledged Promoter Stake and Low Leverage:** Net debt-to-equity is moderate at 0.35x [S2]. Furthermore, promoter share pledging has decreased from 35% in March 2024 [S4] and 11.5% in June 2025 to 4.1% as of June 2026 [S8]. Institutional holdings (FII and DII) also increased sequentially to 11.8% and 14.6%, respectively [S8].

---

### 3. Bear Case
* **Margin Compression and Raw Material Volatility:** Operating margins declined 170 bps YoY to 11.2% due to sharp increases in raw copper prices [S1, S3]. While roughly two-thirds of contracts feature price-escalation clauses, price resets face a one-to-two quarter time lag [S2], leaving margins exposed in the interim.
* **Working Capital Deterioration:** Debtor days deteriorated significantly over the past year, rising from 78 to 96 days, primarily due to delayed payments from two large state utility clients [S2].
* **Tax Contingency Risk:** On 2 September 2026, the company received a CGST demand order totaling ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalties) concerning FY21 and FY22 input tax credit claims [S7]. While the company plans to appeal and disputes the liability, the demand represents over 56% of Q1 FY27 quarterly PAT (₹82 crore) [S3, S7].

---

### 4. Open Questions & Data Discrepancies
* **Conflicting Revenue Figures:** A financial news outlet reported Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,428 crore (+35%) [S1], whereas the company’s official filing reported ₹1,248 crore (+18%) [S3]. The cause of this discrepancy (e.g., media typographical error or alternate accounting scope) is unverified.
* **Receivable Recovery:** Management affirmed no defaults have occurred with state utility clients [S2], but the timeline for recovering overdue receivables and restoring debtor days to historical levels remains undisclosed.
* **Entity Distinction in Legal Reports:** A news report noted an ₹8 lakh penalty against "Sarvottam Cable Network" in Nagpur for unauthorized overhead lines [S6]; this refers to an unlisted local TV/broadband operator owned by a Suresh Patil, not the NSE-listed manufacturer Sarvottam Cables Ltd (headed by MD Anil Deshpande) [S2, S3, S6].
* **Unregulated Speculative Claims:** Social media stock-tip posts have made promotional claims (e.g., multifold upside targets) citing the incorrect 35% growth figure [S5]; these lack official validation and should be disregarded.
